# Privacy Policy for Momentum Arcade

**Last Updated:** October 17, 2026

**Momentum Arcade - New Tab with 10 Games & Lo-Fi** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. Our core philosophy is simple: **your data belongs to you and never leaves your browser.**

## 1. Zero Data Collection
The Extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or share** any personal information, browsing history, analytics, telemetry, or identifiable user data.

## 2. Local Storage Only (`chrome.storage.local`)
The Extension uses Chrome's native local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`) strictly on your own device to save:
- Game high scores and win streaks
- Sound mute/unmute preferences
- Lo-Fi radio volume and mode preferences

This information remains 100% locally on your computer and is never transmitted to any external server.

## 3. Offline Operation & No Third-Party Tracking
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** We do not use Google Analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party telemetry scripts.
- **100% Offline Audio & Games:** All 10 arcade games and the procedural Lo-Fi audio engine run locally inside your browser using native HTML5 Canvas and the Web Audio API.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may open an issue on our official GitHub repository:
