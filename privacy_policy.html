Chrome Web Store Compliance

Privacy Policy for Momentum Arcade

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Momentum Arcade ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to respecting your privacy. Momentum Arcade is a local-first Chrome extension that overrides your New Tab page with a clock, quotes, and casual arcade games.

We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any personal data or browsing activity.

Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, emails, IP addresses, or payment details.
  • No Browsing Activity Tracking: We do not track the websites you visit, search history, bookmarks, or tabs.
  • No Analytics or Third-Party Trackers: No Google Analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, or telemetry scripts are included.

Local Storage API Usage

The extension requests the minimal "storage" permission solely to save your local gameplay data directly on your device via chrome.storage.local:

  • High scores for Snake, Tetris, Block Breaker, and Wordle stats
  • Sound effect preference (enabled/muted)
  • Last selected game tab

All stored preferences remain on your device and are never sent across the internet.

Network Requests

The extension may load daily landscape wallpaper images directly from Bing image services to display scenic backgrounds. No user-identifying queries or cookies are transmitted with these requests.

Single Purpose Compliance

In accordance with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, Momentum Arcade adheres to the Single Purpose policy: providing an aesthetic, productive new tab page featuring offline-accessible casual mini-games.
